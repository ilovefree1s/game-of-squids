Sound files used by Red Light, Green Light

squish_06.mp3 (head-shot impact)
  From "Squish Sounds Effects" on OpenGameArt.org, license CC0 (public domain).
  https://opengameart.org/content/squish-sounds-effects

All other Red Light sounds (the doll's song, music box, crossbow, buzzer, etc.) are made in code.

scream_m0.mp3 - scream_m7.mp3 (contestants screaming near a shot)
  From "aargh male screams" by congusbongus on OpenGameArt.org, license CC-BY 3.0.
  https://opengameart.org/content/aargh-male-screams
  The pack combines "Helmut Scream" (CC-BY 3.0), "Male_Thijs_loud_scream" (CC-BY 3.0) and
  "human male scream multi" (CC0). Converted from .ogg to .mp3 so every browser can play them.

scream_f1.mp3 - scream_f4.mp3 (female screams, for the Rogue, Hooded Rogue and Skeleton Rogue)
  From "female screams" by congusbongus on OpenGameArt.org (page license: CC-BY 3.0; the pack mixes
  CC-BY and CC0 sounds, see license.txt in female_screams.zip for each original author).
  https://opengameart.org/content/female-screams
  Converted from .ogg to .mp3 so every browser can play them.

doll_0.mp3 - doll_9.mp3 (the doll's singing voice)
  Made for this game from the Korean text-to-speech voice "ko_KO kss_low" (Mycroft mimic3-voices, CC0),
  turned into a sung child-like voice. How: see voice-tools/README.txt.

head_servo.mp3 (the doll's head turning)
  "ROBOTIC_Transformation_Insect_01_Servos_Clicks_Digital_stereo.wav" from "Robotic Transformations" by Mekaal on
  OpenGameArt.org. The page lists CC0 among its licenses and says "Use free in your commercial projects."
  https://opengameart.org/content/robotic-transformations
  Converted from .wav to .mp3.
