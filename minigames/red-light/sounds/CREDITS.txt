Sound files used by Red Light, Green Light

squish_06.mp3 (head-shot impact)
  From "Squish Sounds Effects" on OpenGameArt.org, license CC0 (public domain).

scream_m0.mp3 - scream_m7.mp3 (contestants screaming near a shot)
  From "aargh male screams" by congusbongus on OpenGameArt.org, license CC-BY 3.0.
  https://opengameart.org/content/aargh-male-screams
  The pack combines "Helmut Scream" (CC-BY 3.0), "Male_Thijs_loud_scream" (CC-BY 3.0) and
  "human male scream multi" (CC0). Converted from .ogg to .mp3 so every browser can play them.
